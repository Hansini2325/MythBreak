What the platform offers

MythBreak connects learning with real opportunities in one place.

📚
Expert-led courses
Learn from qualified educators. Track your progress and complete courses at your own pace.
📊
Skill match scoring
Our algorithm compares your declared skills against opportunity requirements and gives you a real match percentage.
📁
Portfolio and profile
Add skills, projects, work experience, and links to your profile. Let your portfolio speak for itself.
💼
Jobs and opportunities
Browse full-time jobs, internships, freelance projects, and open-source contributions posted by real companies.
🔔
Application tracking
Track the status of every application. Receive notifications when your status changes.
🔐
Secure and role-based
JWT authentication, BCrypt password hashing, and strict role-based access control protect your account.
